# How to play Richual

Two to six players trade **souls (◈)** for the streets of the Cursed Apple. Buy properties, complete
an organization's set, raise Guardians and Walkers, and bankrupt your rivals before the Patrons
arrive. If you know the classic property-trading game, you already know most of this.

## Your turn

1. **Roll** two dice and move. Doubles let you roll again; three doubles in a row banish you to the
   Lost Whisper.
2. **Resolve the space** you land on:
   - **Unowned property, zipline or utility:** buy it at the printed price, or pass. With auctions
     on, a passed property is auctioned to everyone (you included); bids start at 1 ◈ and each bid
     resets a 20-second clock.
   - **Someone else's property:** pay them rent. Sealed properties charge nothing.
   - **The Oracle / Coven Offering:** draw a card and do what it says.
   - **Patron's Tithe / Ritual Offering:** pay the amount shown.
   - **Banished:** go straight to the Lost Whisper without passing the Astral Gate.
   - **Soul Urn:** with the jackpot rule on, collect every tax and fine paid since it was last
     emptied.
3. **Manage** your holdings (select a property on the board), trade, and **end your turn**.

Passing the **Astral Gate** pays 200 ◈ (double for landing exactly on it, if that rule is on).

## Rent

| Space          | Rent                                                                  |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Property       | Listed rent; doubled if you own the whole set and it has no buildings |
| With buildings | The rent for that many Guardians, or for a Walker                     |
| Zipline        | 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 ◈ for owning 1 / 2 / 3 / 4                        |
| Utility        | 4× the dice for one, 10× for both                                     |

## Building

Own a whole color set with nothing Sealed to build **Guardians** on it, then upgrade to a
**Walker** after four Guardians (three in Quick mode). With even building on, you must build and
sell evenly across the set, and the bank has only 32 Guardians and 12 Walkers. Buildings sell back
for half their cost.

## Sealing

Seal a property (with no buildings in its set) to receive half its price at once. It charges no
rent until you unseal it for that amount plus 10%. Receiving a Sealed property in a trade costs 10%
of its seal value in interest.

## The Lost Whisper

While banished you can pay a 50 ◈ fine, use an escape card, or try for doubles. Doubles free you
and you move, with no extra roll. After a third failed attempt you must pay the fine and move.
You still collect rent while banished.

## Trades

Any player can offer souls, properties and escape cards to another at any time, except during an
auction. Properties whose set has buildings can't be traded. An offer that can no longer be honored
is withdrawn automatically.

## Debts and bankruptcy

If you owe more than you hold but could cover it by selling buildings and sealing, you must raise
the souls and pay, or declare bankruptcy. If even that wouldn't cover it, you are bankrupt at once.
A bankrupt player's holdings go to the player they owed, or are auctioned if they owed the bank.

## Winning

| Mode                    | Ends when                                                | Winner                          |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Classic                 | One player remains                                       | The last player standing        |
| Quick                   | The second bankruptcy                                    | Highest net worth               |
| Any, with a round limit | That many rounds are complete                            | Highest net worth               |
| Any, with a time limit  | Active play time runs out and the current round finishes | Highest net worth               |
| Ended by the host       | Immediately                                              | Highest net worth (provisional) |

**Net worth** = souls + the price of each property you own (half if Sealed) + what your buildings
cost to build. Ties go to the player with more souls.

**Quick mode** deals two random properties to each player at the start (paid for at their printed
price), allows a Walker after three Guardians, turns off the cash-adding house rules, and defaults to
a 20-round limit.

## Waiting in queue

- The **host** can pause and resume (clocks stop while paused; trade offers can still be made) and
  end the game early.
- An optional **turn timer** auto-plays idle players with safe moves only: roll, pass, end the turn,
  and sell or seal just enough to pay a debt. It never trades.
- If the host is away for two minutes, hosting passes to the player who has been connected longest.
- Reconnect any time from the same browser: your seat is kept.
